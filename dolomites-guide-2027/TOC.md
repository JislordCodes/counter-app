## DECISIONS FOR THE AUTHOR

1. **Length.** The master prompt asks for more depth than 150 pages comfortably holds. The plan above fits about 42,000 words in 150–170 pages with dense At-a-Glance boxes. If you want every framework item for every attraction in full sentences, plan for about 200 pages.
2. **Title and author name** for the cover and title page.
3. **Update page.** A free web page linked by QR code lets you post 2027 changes after printing. This is the single best defence against out-of-date reviews.
4. **Personal experience.** Where the master prompt says "sound like an experienced local guide," any first-person experience must be yours. I will leave marked spots for your own tips and photos.
