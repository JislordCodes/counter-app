# 3. Book Ahead: Reservations, Access Rules and Entry Requirements

A few years ago, you could wake up, look at the sky and drive to Lago di Braies. In 2026, that same plan could cost you a €40 parking fee or a turned-away car. The best-known places in the Dolomites now manage crowds with timed tickets, reserved parking, road closures and shuttle buses.

This chapter tells you which places have rules, how the systems worked in 2026, and when to book. It also covers the entry rules you need to check before you fly.

> **Important:** Everything here is based on the **2026 season**. The 2027 rules will be published later, usually in spring. They are likely to be similar, but dates, prices and time slots can change. Use the official links in Appendix C to confirm.

## The book-ahead rule of thumb

**Book anything with limited capacity. Stay flexible about anything that depends on weather.**

Limited-capacity items include timed lift tickets, car access to a lake or a toll road, beds in a mountain hut, and hotel rooms in peak weeks. Weather-dependent items include your main hiking day, which is better chosen the evening before.

## Seceda lifts (Ortisei)

| At a glance | |
|---|---|
| **What it is** | Gondola from Ortisei to Furnes, then cable car to Seceda (about 2,500 m), the famous ridge above Val Gardena. |
| **Why visit** | The most photographed view in Val Gardena. Suits walkers of any level and anyone who wants high-mountain views without a hard climb. |
| **Access** | Lift station in Ortisei. Parking is limited, so use the bus if you can. |
| **2026 price** | Combined round trip €74 adult, €37 junior (8–15), free for children under 8. Dog €7. |
| **2026 season** | 22 May to 2 November, about 08:30 to 17:30. Check the exact hours on the day. |
| **How to book** | Buy online at seceda.it. In 2026 one source reported timed 30-minute slots and an early-booking discount. Confirm for 2027. |
| **Crowd reality** | Busy from late morning to mid-afternoon in summer. The ridge thins out once you walk away from the lift. |
| **Typical visit** | 2 to 4 hours, longer if you add a hut lunch. |
| **Common mistake** | Turning up at midday in August and finding the next slots full or the queue long. |
| **Potential drawback** | It is expensive for a short ride, and it is crowded. |

> **Wish I'd Known:** In 2026, the Dolomiti SuperSummer card did not cover the Seceda lifts. The Gardena Card did. If Seceda is on your list, compare the two before you buy.

## Lago di Braies (Pragser Wildsee)

| At a glance | |
|---|---|
| **What it is** | A famous lake at 1,496 m, and the official start of the Alta Via 1 trek. |
| **Restriction (2026)** | From 1 July to 15 September, 09:00–16:00, you cannot drive to the lake without a reservation or permit. Outside those hours, and after 15 September, there was no restriction. |
| **Parking (2026)** | P4 by the lake: €40, pre-booked only (pragsparking.com). P3 (300 m): about €20 pay on site, €25–44 reserved. P2 (800 m): about €13, or €44 reserved. P1 park-and-ride (5.5 km) with a free shuttle every 30 minutes. |
| **Buses (2026)** | Lines 442 and 439 required online reservation in the restricted period. |
| **Typical visit** | 1 to 2 hours for the lake walk, which is about an hour at an easy pace. Longer with a boat or hut stop. |
| **Boats (2026)** | €55 for 45 minutes (private, up to 5), or about €20 per person shared. Opening hours about 08:00–19:00. |
| **Crowd reality** | Very busy from mid-morning. Arriving before 09:00 or after 16:00 is the best way to avoid the worst. |
| **Drones** | Banned. The lake lies in the Fanes-Sennes-Braies Nature Park, where drone flights have been banned since 2023 (Chapter 17). |
| **Common mistake** | Driving up at 10:00 without a reservation and being turned back. |

**The options, ranked by effort:**

1. **Arrive before 09:00.** Free, but you need an early start and may face parking fees.
2. **Take the bus** (with a reservation in the restricted period).
3. **Use P1 park-and-ride** with the free shuttle.
4. **Reserve P4** if you want to park beside the lake.

## Tre Cime di Lavaredo toll road

| At a glance | |
|---|---|
| **What it is** | A 6–7 km toll road to Rifugio Auronzo (2,333 m), the main start for the Tre Cime loop walk. |
| **2026 price** | €40 car, €26 motorbike, €60 camper. |
| **How to book** | Online at the operator's site (pass.auronzo.info). Booking opened about 30 days ahead in 2026. |
| **Time slot** | A fixed 12-hour window, for example 06:00–18:00. One entry only; no re-entry. Leave before your window ends or you pay extra. |
| **2026 season** | Provisionally from 23 May to late October, depending on snow. |
| **Loop walk** | About 9.5–10 km, 3–4 hours, easy to moderate. |
| **Alternatives** | Shuttle 444 from Dobbiaco station (2026: €22 round trip, reservation mandatory, last return about 18:08, guest passes not valid). Misurina shuttle (about €10 a day in 2026). Walk from Lake Antorno or Malga Rin Bianco (about 4 km, under 2 hours each way) or up Val Fiscalina from Sesto. See Chapter 10. |
| **Overnight** | Vehicles can stay overnight with two consecutive slots; camping is not allowed. |
| **Common mistake** | Arriving without a booking, or booking a slot and not leaving inside the window. |

> **Wish I'd Known:** The booking window opens about a month before your date. For popular July and August days, set a reminder and book on day one.

## Alpe di Siusi (Seiser Alm)

| At a glance | |
|---|---|
| **What it is** | One of Europe's biggest high-altitude meadows, reached by cable car or by restricted road. |
| **Cars (2026)** | Private cars allowed only before 09:00 and after 17:00. An access-permit checkpoint at St. Valentin applies from 29 June 2026. Downhill travel is allowed at any time. Hotel guests get permits from their host. |
| **Parking (2026)** | P1 Spitzbühl: €15 a day, with a 30-minute walk to Compatsch. P2 Compatsch: €30 a day. Reservations are required from 29 June 2026, up to 6 days ahead. No parking 23:00–05:00. |
| **Cable car** | From Siusi (Seis), with free parking at the base. About €30 adult round trip in 2026 (secondary source). |
| **Bus** | Line 14 from P1 to Compatsch, 25 July to 6 September, €4 (free with a lift ticket). |
| **Winter and shoulder** | The road is open all day in mid-April to mid-May and early November to early December. |
| **Common mistake** | Driving up at 11:00 without a permit and getting fined. |

## Other places to check

- **Lago di Carezza.** No entry fee and no reservation in 2026. Parking at P1 was €3 an hour, up to €30 a day.
- **Passo Gardena, Sella, Pordoi and Campolongo (the Sella Ronda passes).** No booking, but **closures on bike days.** In 2026 the four passes were closed to cars and motorbikes from 08:30 to 16:00 on Sellaronda Bike Day (6 June and 12 September). The 2027 first date is reported as 5 June.
- **Passo Gardena.** A planned traffic limit was dropped in 2026 in favour of a digital test phase. Check for updates.
- **Falzarego, Valparola and Campolongo.** Closed to motor traffic during some bike events (for example, 20 June 2026).
- **Cinque Torri and Lagazuoi lifts.** No booking reported. Lifts have fixed seasons and queues in peak weeks.
- **Marmolada.** Summit-glacier access is only possible with a certified guide after the 2022 glacier collapse. The cable car to Punta Rocca (3,265 m) operates on a seasonal schedule.
- **Motorbikes.** In June 2026, South Tyrol adopted rules against organised motorcycle events on some mountain passes above 1,600 m. Normal private riding stays allowed, but check local limits.

## Mountain huts

Huts (*rifugi*) are small and fill up. Most Alta Via 1 huts open reservations in January, and some from October of the previous year. Methods vary: some have online booking, some use email or phone only.

- Expect to pay about €60–110 per person per night with half board (dinner and breakfast), depending on room type.
- Bring a sleeping bag liner, towel, indoor shoes and earplugs.
- Carry some cash. Many huts accept cards, but some do not.
- If dates are full, check again one or two months before your trip. Cancellations happen.

## Booking timeline

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| **9–12 months before** | Book hotels for July, August, Christmas and February. Decide your region. |
| **6–9 months before** | Book flights, trains and rental car for peak weeks. Start planning huts. |
| **January of your travel year** | Book Alta Via 1 and other hut stays as reservations open. |
| **Spring** | Check the 2027 lift and pass dates. Buy lift passes if you have a fixed plan. |
| **30 days before** | Book the Tre Cime toll road (when the window opens). |
| **2–4 weeks before** | Book Seceda lifts and Braies parking or buses when they open. |
| **1 week before** | Reserve Alpe di Siusi parking if you will drive. Check weather and open dates. |
| **Night before** | Choose your hike. Confirm lifts, huts and parking. |

## What you can leave open

- Most hiking trails.
- Public buses (check timetables).
- Lunch at a hut (reserve only for very popular spots in peak weeks).
- Most restaurants outside peak weeks. Book fine dining ahead.
- Your single best-weather day.

## Entry requirements for 2027

Rules for entering Italy change often. Check these before you book flights.

- **Passport.** It should be valid for at least three months after you leave the Schengen area and issued within the past ten years.
- **90-day rule.** Visitors from visa-exempt countries can stay up to 90 days in any 180-day period.
- **EES (Entry/Exit System).** It became fully operational on 10 April 2026. On your first crossing of an external Schengen border you give fingerprints and a face photo at a kiosk or booth. You do nothing in advance.
- **ETIAS.** This is a travel authorisation, not a visa, for visitors from visa-exempt countries (including the United States). The fee is reported as €20 (those under 18 and over 70 are exempt). Earlier EU announcements pointed to the last quarter of 2026, while more recent reports say it becomes mandatory in 2027. The start date has moved before. Check **travel-europe.europa.eu** before you fly.
- **Driving licence.** Many non-EU drivers need an International Driving Permit as well as their licence. Check with your rental company.
- **Insurance.** Make sure it covers mountain rescue. See Chapter 19.

## Common mistakes

- Waiting until you arrive to ask about Seceda tickets.
- Reading 2026 prices as final 2027 prices.
- Assuming that a lift pass covers every lift.
- Reserving parking for the wrong day or lot.
- Forgetting that some systems only open about a month ahead.
