# PART 2: EXPLORE THE REGIONS

# 7. Val Gardena and Alpe di Siusi

Val Gardena (Gröden, Gherdëina) and the Alpe di Siusi (Seiser Alm) are the easiest places to start a first Dolomites trip. Lifts, buses, hotels and restaurants are close together, and the area holds several of the best-known views in the range: the Seceda ridge, the Sassolungo group and the wide meadows of the Alpe di Siusi.

It is also one of the busiest areas, and it has more access rules than any other. This chapter shows you how to enjoy it without the common mistakes.

## Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Province** | South Tyrol |
| **Main villages** | Ortisei (St. Ulrich), Santa Cristina, Selva (Wolkenstein); on the meadows: Compatsch, Siusi, Castelrotto |
| **Language** | Ladin, German, Italian |
| **Best for** | First-timers, families, couples, walkers of all levels, skiers |
| **Car needed?** | Helpful, not essential. Buses and lifts work well in season. |
| **Nearest airport** | Bolzano (about 45 minutes); Verona, Innsbruck and Venice are further |
| **Season** | Summer lifts about late May to early November; winter from December |

## Getting there and getting around

- **From Bolzano.** SAD bus 350 runs between Bolzano and Ortisei in about 50 to 60 minutes, every 30 to 60 minutes depending on the season (2026 information).
- **By car.** Leave the A22 at Chiusa/Klausen and follow the valley road. Expect heavy traffic on summer weekends.
- **Within the valley.** Buses link Ortisei, Santa Cristina and Selva. Many hotels give guests a **Val Gardena Guest Pass** for free public transport. Ask when you book.
- **Lift pass.** The **Gardena Card** (2026: 3 days €124 or 6 days €160, valid 6 June to 11 October) covers 17 lifts, including Seceda. The SuperSummer card did not include Seceda in 2026 (Chapter 4).

## Top sights

### 1. Seceda

| At a glance | |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | A ridge above Ortisei at about 2,500 m, with the jagged Odle (Geisler) peaks behind it. The best-known viewpoint in Val Gardena. |
| **Why visit** | Big views for little effort. Good for walkers of any fitness and for photographers. |
| **Location and access** | Gondola from Ortisei to Furnes, then cable car to Seceda. Or from Santa Cristina by cable car to Col Raiser and walk. |
| **Travel time** | Two lift stages. Allow about an hour from the village, including queues. |
| **Visit time** | 2 to 4 hours. A full day if you walk a loop. |
| **Cost (2026)** | Combined lifts €74 adult round trip. Col Raiser cable car about €34. A **€5 turnstile** was reported on the ridge path to the best-known viewpoint (see below). |
| **Best time** | Afternoon for light. Morning for fewer people. Summer and early autumn. |
| **Crowd reality** | Busy from late morning to mid-afternoon in summer. The crowd thins out as you walk away from the lift. |
| **Difficulty** | Easy. The ridge walk is 1.3 km, about 30 to 45 minutes, with about 110 m of climbing. Longer loops are moderate. |
| **Photography** | Afternoon sun lights the peaks. Morning light can be backlit. |
| **Practical tips** | Book lifts ahead (Chapter 3). Wear proper shoes: the paths are gravel and rock. Walk beyond the first viewpoint for space. |
| **Common mistakes** | Arriving at midday in August. Planning a long hike late in the day. Going up in poor visibility. |
| **Nearby** | Baita Sofie hut and the restaurant at the upper station for food. Col Raiser. Val di Funes (below). |
| **Drawbacks** | Expensive, crowded, and the most photographed spot has its own fee. |

> **Wish I'd Known:** Private landowners in the Puez-Odle Nature Park installed a paid turnstile (reported at €5) on the path toward the famous postcard viewpoint. In 2026, a second collection point was reported. A bypass trail gives a nearly equal view a few metres further on. The Panascharte saddle itself is closed. Fees and rules can change, so check on the day.

**Two ways to do Seceda**

- **Short:** Ride up from Ortisei, walk the ridge (about 30 to 45 minutes), have lunch, ride down.
- **Longer:** Ride from Santa Cristina to Col Raiser (2,107 m) and walk the loop to Seceda and back. The loop was listed at about 9.7 km, around 4 hours, with 570 m of climbing, moderate, passing the Pieralongia and Firenze huts. It avoids the Ortisei lift queues and gives a quiet return through meadows.

### 2. Alpe di Siusi (Seiser Alm)

| At a glance | |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | One of Europe's largest high-altitude meadows, with the Sciliar (Schlern) and Sassolungo as backdrops. |
| **Why visit** | Gentle walking at altitude, huts with views, and a good choice for families and for days with less energy. |
| **Location and access** | Cable car from Siusi (Seis), or restricted road (Chapter 3). Bus line 14 from P1 in peak summer. |
| **Travel time** | Siusi to Compatsch by cable car is a short ride. Drive times to Selva about 45 minutes, Ortisei about 35, Bolzano about 45 (earthtrekkers estimates). |
| **Visit time** | Half a day to a full day. |
| **Cost (2026)** | Cable car about €30 adult round trip. Parking at Compatsch €30 a day; at Spitzbühl €15 a day. Florian chairlift €20 round trip. |
| **Best time** | Early morning and late afternoon. Summer for flowers; early autumn for quiet. |
| **Crowd reality** | Busiest 10:00 to 16:00. Quiet outside those hours. |
| **Difficulty** | Easy to moderate. Rolling meadows, not steep. |
| **Photography** | Afternoon and evening give the best light on the Sassolungo and Sciliar. |
| **Practical tips** | Hire an e-bike if you want to cover distance on rolling terrain. Bus back from Saltria instead of hiking uphill. |
| **Common mistakes** | Driving up at 11:00 and being fined. Leaving the return bus too late. Not checking the cable car's last descent. |
| **Nearby** | Sciliar, Castelrotto, Val di Funes, Seceda. |
| **Drawbacks** | Access rules are strict. Busy at midday. |

**Walks on the meadows**

| Walk | Distance and time | Level | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compatsch to Saltria | 6.8 km, about 2 hours | Easy | Passes Sanon hut (about 50 minutes from Compatsch). Return by bus. |
| Florian chairlift to Williamshütte | Chairlift €20 round trip | Easy | Playground; hut with Sassolungo views. |
| Sasso Piatto | About 2 hours up from Williamshütte | Strenuous | For fit walkers. |
| Sciliar (Schlern) circuit | About 22.7 km, +1,133 m | Moderate to hard (T2) | Schlernhaus at 2,457 m. About 3.5 hours from Compatsch to the hut. Needs sure feet. |

### 3. Sassolungo and Passo Sella

| At a glance | |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | A massive group of towers rising above the pass road between Val Gardena and Val di Fassa. |
| **Why visit** | Dramatic scenery close to the road. The circuit walk is one of the best day walks in the region. |
| **Location and access** | Passo Sella, about 2,200 m. By car, or bus 350/352 to Plan de Gralba then bus 471 (every 15 minutes in 2026) to the pass. |
| **Travel time** | From Selva, a short drive or bus ride. |
| **Visit time** | The circuit is 6 hours walking, about 8 with stops. |
| **Cost (2026)** | Forcella Sassolungo cable car €30 one way, €45 round trip. Parking at Passo Sella is paid and varies by lot (about €20 a day at one; €8 a day was listed near Rifugio Valentini). |
| **Best time** | Summer and early autumn. Start early. |
| **Crowd reality** | The pass is busy with cars, bikes and motorbikes. Parking fills by mid-morning. |
| **Difficulty** | Circuit: moderate to hard (17.6 km, about 1,000 m up and down). Cable car options are shorter but the descent can be steep and technical. |
| **Photography** | Late afternoon on the walls. |
| **Practical tips** | Use the bus on busy days. Take water and layers. The Rifugio Vicenza detour adds about 300 m of climbing. Skip it if tired. |
| **Common mistakes** | Trying the whole circuit with a late start. Underestimating the descent from Forcella Sassolungo. |
| **Nearby** | Sella Ronda passes (Chapter 8), Selva, Passo Gardena. |
| **Drawbacks** | Traffic, parking and a long day for the full circuit. |

> **Wish I'd Known:** The Forcella Sassolungo cable car has tiny two-person cabins that look like upright coffins. They are not for everyone. Capacity is small, so queues can be long.

### 4. Val di Funes and Santa Maddalena

| At a glance | |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | A quiet side valley beside Val Gardena. The Santa Maddalena (St. Magdalena) church with the Odle peaks behind is one of the most photographed views in South Tyrol. |
| **Why visit** | The famous church view, and a gentler alternative to Seceda. |
| **Location and access** | Val di Funes (Villnöss), off the valley road near Chiusa. |
| **Cost (2026)** | Parking reported at about €15 a space (earlier reports gave €10; check the current price). |
| **Rules (2026)** | From mid-May to November, a barrier limits the village to residents and overnight guests. Day visitors park at designated areas and walk. Reports gave walks of about 15 minutes to 30 minutes or more. Coaches need online booking. |
| **Crowd reality** | Up to about 600 people a day at peak times, in a village of about 2,500 residents. |
| **Best time** | Early morning or late afternoon. |
| **Common mistakes** | Driving up for a quick photo and being turned away. Walking onto private fields or farm land. |
| **Drawbacks** | The access rules are new and may change. |

> **Wish I'd Known:** Reports differ on the exact walking time and parking price in Santa Maddalena, and the municipality had not decided on a shuttle bus. Check the Val di Funes tourist office before you go. Stay on the path and out of the meadows. Locals have complained of visitors walking onto private land.

### 5. Ortisei and the wood-carving tradition

| At a glance | |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | The main village of Val Gardena, known for four centuries of wood carving. |
| **Why visit** | A useful bad-weather option and a break from the mountains. |
| **Museum Gherdëina** | €8 adult, €6 reduced, €16 family, €2.50 students under 26. Summer hours (15 May to 13 October): Monday to Friday 10:00 to 12:30 and 14:00 to 18:00; Saturday 10:00 to 12:30 (1 July to 31 August). |
| **Other rainy-day options** | Mar Dolomit pool with 7 pools and 11 saunas in Ortisei; climbing and bouldering halls in Selva and Ortisei; ice rink at Pranives in Selva; indoor tennis; an escape room in Selva. |
| **Visit time** | The museum takes 1 to 2 hours. |

## Where to stay

See Chapter 6. For a first visit, **Ortisei** (lifts, restaurants, buses) or **Santa Cristina** (quieter, Col Raiser lift) work best. **Selva** suits Sassolungo and the Sella passes. On the meadows, **Compatsch** and **Siusi** give early and late access without the day crowd.

## Where to eat

- **Mountain huts.** Baita Sofie near Seceda, Sanon hut on the meadows and the huts at Williamshütte all serve meals with views. Carry some cash.
- **Restaurants in the villages.** Ortisei, Santa Cristina and Selva all have a good choice of local and Italian restaurants. Book dinner in July and August.
- **Local dishes to try.** See Chapter 16.

## Photography notes

- **Seceda.** Afternoon light on the Odle. Walk past the first crowd.
- **Alpe di Siusi.** Late afternoon and evening light on the Sassolungo and Sciliar.
- **Santa Maddalena.** Early morning or late afternoon, within the access rules. Stay out of private fields.
- **Drones.** Seceda lies in the Puez-Odle Nature Park, where drone flights are banned. See Chapter 17.

## Rainy-day options

Museum Gherdëina, the Mar Dolomit pool, climbing halls, the ice rink and indoor shops in the pedestrian streets. If the clouds lift, a low walk on the meadows is still possible.

## Common mistakes in this region

- Driving to the Alpe di Siusi in the middle of the day.
- Booking one day for Seceda, Alpe di Siusi and Sassolungo together.
- Not checking the last cable car down.
- Ignoring the paid turnstile at Seceda or the barrier at Santa Maddalena.
- Assuming the SuperSummer card covers Seceda.

## A simple plan for three days

This is a short plan. Chapter 18 has full itineraries.

- **Day 1:** Seceda in the morning with the Col Raiser or ridge walk. Rest in the afternoon.
- **Day 2:** Alpe di Siusi by cable car. Walk Compatsch to Saltria and bus back.
- **Day 3:** Sassolungo and Passo Sella by bus, with the circuit if the weather is stable. Backup: Museum Gherdëina and the pool.
