# PART 5: ITINERARIES

# 18. Ready-to-Follow Itineraries

These plans are built from the facts in the earlier chapters: lift hours, booking windows, walking times and access rules. You can follow them as written or use them as a starting point.

## How to use these itineraries

- **Times are guides.** Lift hours, bus timetables and booking rules come from the 2026 season. Confirm them for 2027 before you travel (Chapter 3 and Appendix A).
- **Drive times are estimates.** They depend on traffic, weather and the day of the week. Check your map app and add a buffer in July and August.
- **Every plan has a weather swap.** Mountain weather changes fast. Keep a flexible day if you can.
- **Costs are 2026 prices** for lifts and entry, per person, excluding hotels, meals, parking and transport to the region.
- **Each plan lists:** the base, a day-by-day schedule, a budget version, a family version, an optional upgrade and the most common mistake.

## 1. Three days in Val Gardena (one base, car optional)

**Base:** Ortisei. **Season:** late June to mid-October. **Lifts and entry (2026 prices):** Seceda €74, Alpe di Siusi cable car about €30, Forcella Sassolungo cable car €45 (optional).

### Day 1: Seceda

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| 07:45 | Breakfast. Book your Seceda lift slot the week before (Chapter 3). |
| 08:20 | Walk or take the bus to the Ortisei lift station. Parking is limited. |
| 08:30–09:30 | Gondola to Furnes, then cable car to Seceda. Allow up to an hour with queues. The lifts open about 08:30. |
| 09:30–11:30 | Ridge walk (1.3 km, 30 to 45 minutes each way) and viewpoints. Walk past the first crowd. Have a card ready if you plan to use the paid turnstile (reported at €5, paid electronically), or take the bypass trail. |
| 11:30–12:30 | Lunch at Baita Sofie or the upper-station restaurant. |
| 13:00–14:00 | Ride down. Last lift about 17:30. |
| 14:00–18:00 | Rest, or visit the Museum Gherdëina (Monday to Friday 14:00 to 18:00; €8). |

- **Weather swap:** If it is cloudy at 08:00, stay in the valley. Museum, Mar Dolomit pool or a low walk. Try Seceda on the first clear day.
- **Common mistake:** Arriving at 11:30 in August with no booking.

### Day 2: Alpe di Siusi

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| 08:30 | Bus or car to Siusi (Seis). About 35 minutes by car from Ortisei. If you drive to the meadows, you must be up before 09:00 (Chapter 3). |
| 09:30 | Cable car up (it ran about 08:00 to 19:00 in 2026). |
| 10:00–12:00 | Walk Compatsch to Saltria (6.8 km, about 2 hours, easy). Stop at Sanon hut (about 50 minutes from Compatsch). |
| 12:30–13:30 | Lunch at Sanon hut or Saltria. |
| 14:00 | Bus from Saltria back to Compatsch. Do not hike uphill. |
| 15:00–17:00 | Easy walk near Compatsch, or the Florian chairlift (€20 return). Take the cable car down before it closes. |

- **Weather swap:** Low cloud on the meadows is common. Visit the Museum Gherdëina and Mar Dolomit pool, or choose a shorter walk below the cloud.
- **Common mistake:** Driving up at 11:00 and getting fined, or leaving the last cable car too late.

### Day 3: Sassolungo and Passo Sella (clear day only)

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| 08:00 | Bus 350 or 352 to Plan de Gralba. |
| 08:30 | Bus 471 to Passo Sella (every 15 minutes in 2026). |
| 09:00–16:00 | **Option A (short):** Forcella Sassolungo cable car (€45 round trip) and walk near the top. **Option B (full):** the Sassolungo circuit, 17.6 km and about 6 hours of walking (8 with stops). Start by 08:30. |
| 17:00 | Bus back to Selva or Ortisei. Check the last bus before you set out. |

- **Weather swap:** If clouds hang on the rocks, spend the day at the Mar Dolomit pool (7 pools, 11 saunas), a climbing hall or the ice rink in Selva.
- **Common mistake:** Starting the full circuit after 10:00.

### Options for the three days

| Version | Change |
|---|---|
| **Budget** | Skip the Forcella Sassolungo cable car. Use the Val Gardena Guest Pass for buses (ask your hotel). Make lunch the main meal. |
| **Family** | Day 2 on the meadows (playgrounds, stroller paths). Day 1: Col Raiser cable car instead of Seceda, with a short walk. Day 3: museum, pool or Adolf Munkel Weg (easy loop of 5 to 7 km). |
| **Upgrade** | Add a tandem paragliding flight at Seceda or Spitzbühl (about €90 to €130, weather permitting). |

## 2. Five days: Val Gardena and Cortina (car recommended)

**Bases:** Ortisei (2 nights), Cortina (3 nights). **Lifts and entry (2026 prices):** Seceda €74, Alpe di Siusi cable car about €30, Lagazuoi cable car €29.50, Cinque Torri chairlift about €15, Tre Cime access €10 (bus) or €40 (car).

### Day 1: Seceda (Ortisei)

Follow Day 1 of the three-day plan. Rest in the afternoon.

### Day 2: Alpe di Siusi, then to Cortina

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| 08:00 | Check out. Cable car from Siusi, or drive up before 09:00. Do not leave valuables in the car. |
| 09:30–12:30 | Compatsch to Saltria walk (about 2 hours) and lunch at Sanon hut. |
| 14:30 | Drive from Siusi to Cortina over the Passo Gardena, Passo Campolongo and Passo Falzarego. Allow about 2.5 to 3 hours with stops. Avoid Sellaronda Bike Day. |
| 18:00 | Arrive in Cortina. Check the parking and the ZTL with your hotel before you drive into town. |

- **Weather swap:** If the weather is poor, skip the meadows and drive via Alta Badia, stopping at the Ladin museum in San Martino (Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 to 18:00).
- **Common mistake:** Booking a hotel in the ZTL without checking how to reach it.

### Day 3: Cinque Torri and Lagazuoi

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| 08:30 | Drive to the Cinque Torri chairlift (the lift opened on 6 June in 2026). Parking about €5 to €10; carry cash. |
| 09:00–12:30 | Chairlift, open-air museum, Rifugio Scoiattoli. Optional steep 1 km walk to Rifugio Nuvolau. |
| 13:00 | Lunch at Scoiattoli. |
| 14:30–17:30 | Passo Falzarego and the Lagazuoi cable car (€29.50). If you want the tunnels, you need a helmet and headtorch (rental about €5) and nearly 3 hours. Start earlier in that case. |

- **Weather swap:** If the Lagazuoi tunnels are wet or cloud is low, skip them and take the Passo Giau drive instead (check parking signs).
- **Common mistake:** Entering the tunnels in trainers or without a headtorch.

### Day 4: Tre Cime

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| 07:30 | Take bus 51 from Cortina to Misurina, then the shuttle to Rifugio Auronzo (about €10 each way in 2026; confirm the price). Or drive with a toll road booking (€40; book about 30 days ahead). |
| 09:30–15:00 | Tre Cime loop (9.5 to 10 km, 3.5 to 4 hours), with a lunch stop at Rifugio Locatelli. Optional Cadini di Misurina viewpoint (about 1.5 hours round trip). |
| 17:00 | Shuttle and bus back. Last shuttle from Misurina about 19:15 (2026). |

- **Weather swap:** Check the forecast the night before. If the mountain is in cloud, visit Lago di Misurina and Monte Piana, or return to Cortina for shops and a long lunch.
- **Common mistake:** Overstaying a toll road window, or starting after midday.

### Day 5: Lago di Braies and departure

| Time | Plan |
|---|---|
| 07:00 | Drive toward Braies via Dobbiaco. Allow about 1 to 1.5 hours. You need to arrive before 09:00 during the summer restrictions (1 July to 15 September in 2026). |
| 08:30–10:30 | Lake walk (about 1 hour). Boat if you want it (about €55 for 45 minutes private). |
| 11:00 | Drive to Dobbiaco (about 20 minutes). Train from Dobbiaco toward Brunico, Bressanone and Bolzano. |

- **Weather swap:** If it rains, spend the morning in Dobbiaco and take the train early.
- **Common mistake:** Arriving at 10:00 without a reservation.

### Options for the five days

| Version | Change |
|---|---|
| **Budget** | Take buses and the free guest pass. Skip Lagazuoi's cable car and walk around Cinque Torri. Stay in Dobbiaco or Sesto rather than Cortina. |
| **Family** | Replace Sorapis and the Lagazuoi tunnels with Cinque Torri (playground), Passo Giau views and the boat at Braies. |
| **Upgrade** | Add Lago di Sorapis (10.5 km, about 4 hours, exposed) in place of a Cortina day, if the weather is dry. |

## 3. Seven days: three bases

**Bases:** Ortisei (3 nights), Cortina (2 nights), Sesto or Dobbiaco (2 nights).

| Day | Plan | Backup |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Seceda (Day 1 of the three-day plan) | Museum Gherdëina, pool |
| **2** | Alpe di Siusi (Day 2 of the three-day plan) | Pool, climbing hall |
| **3** | Sassolungo and Passo Sella (clear day) or the Adolf Munkel Weg in Val di Funes | Ice rink at Pranives (Selva), museum |
| **4** | Drive over the four passes to Cortina (about 2.5 to 3 hours with stops). Cinque Torri chairlift in the afternoon | Ladin museum in San Martino |
| **5** | Lagazuoi tunnels (early start, helmet, headtorch) and Passo Falzarego | Passo Giau drive, Cortina town |
| **6** | Tre Cime loop (shuttle or toll road) and sleep in Sesto or Dobbiaco | Monte Piana, Lago di Misurina |
| **7** | Lago di Braies before 09:00, then train from Dobbiaco | Dobbiaco cycle path (Dobbiaco to Cortina, about 33 km) |

**Lifts and entry:** about €74 + €30 + €45 (optional) + €15 + €29.50 + €10 to €40 = about €205 to €235.

- **Budget:** Skip Forcella Sassolungo and the Lagazuoi cable car. Base in Fassa instead of Cortina for lower prices.
- **Family:** Replace Day 5 with a Cinque Torri morning and a pool or museum afternoon.
- **Upgrade:** Add the Val Fiscalina walk to Rifugio Locatelli (about 5 hours, hard) instead of the Tre Cime loop.
- **Common mistake:** Moving bases on a day you also plan a big walk.

## 4. Ten days: the Dolomites highlights

**Bases:** Bolzano (1 night), Ortisei (3), Val di Fassa (2), Cortina (2), Sesto (2).

| Day | Base | Plan | Backup |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Bolzano | Arrive. Ötzi Museum (€13), old town, optional Renon cableway | Museums, café stops |
| **2** | Ortisei | Bus 350 to Ortisei. Seceda in the afternoon if the lift slots allow, or rest | Pool |
| **3** | Ortisei | Alpe di Siusi | Museum Gherdëina |
| **4** | Ortisei | Sassolungo and Passo Sella | Pool, climbing hall |
| **5** | Fassa | Over Passo Sella to Canazei. Col Rodella or Gardeccia in the afternoon | Museums, spa |
| **6** | Fassa | Vajolet chairlift, Gardeccia and the Vajolet Towers (full day); Marmolada cable car on a second clear morning | Carezza lake, Passo Pordoi museum (€6) |
| **7** | Cortina | Cinque Torri and Lagazuoi | Passo Giau drive |
| **8** | Cortina | Lago di Sorapis (dry weather, experienced walkers) or Passo Giau | Cortina town |
| **9** | Sesto | Tre Cime loop | Monte Piana |
| **10** | Sesto | Braies before 09:00, then Dobbiaco train | Cycle path |

- **Budget:** Use guest passes (Val Gardena and Val di Fassa) for buses. Prefer Fassa and Sesto bases.
- **Family:** Replace Sorapis with Cinque Torri and the Stadl Alm walk from Lago di Carezza.
- **Upgrade:** Add a guided half-day via ferrata at Passo Gardena (about €150 per person for a group).
- **Common mistake:** Trying to see every region without two buffer days for weather.

## 5. Special versions

### Car-free version (seven days)

**Bases:** Ortisei (3 nights), Dobbiaco (4 nights). You need a transport pass or guest pass.

| Day | Plan |
|---|---|
| **1–3** | Val Gardena by lift and bus: Seceda, Alpe di Siusi (cable car), Sassolungo via bus 350/352 and 471 |
| **4** | Bus 350 to Bolzano, train via Fortezza to Dobbiaco (check connections) |
| **5** | Shuttle 444 from Dobbiaco station to Rifugio Auronzo (€22 return, reservation mandatory, last return about 18:08; Mobilcard and guest passes not valid). Tre Cime loop |
| **6** | Bus 442 to Lago di Braies (reservation needed 1 July to 15 September in 2026). Be at the lake early |
| **7** | Dobbiaco to Cortina cycle path (about 33 km, easy, rent an e-bike) or a rest day in San Candido |

**Common mistake:** Not reserving the shuttle and bus seats.

### Budget version (seven days)

- Base in Fassa (Canazei) and Sesto, with a Val di Fassa Guest Card and local buses.
- Make lunch your main meal and buy picnic items in valley supermarkets.
- Choose free or low-cost activities: Alpe di Siusi meadows (cable car about €30), Lago di Braies (arrive before 09:00), Cinque Torri open-air museum (free; chairlift about €15), the Dobbiaco to Cortina cycle path.
- Skip: Seceda, Forcella Sassolungo, Lagazuoi cable car.
- **Common mistake:** Booking half board and then eating out.

### Photographer's version (five days)

| Day | Plan | Light |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Stay near the lifts. Seceda by mid-morning; walk past the crowd | Afternoon or sunrise (opinions differ; see Chapter 17) |
| **2** | Alpe di Siusi at sunrise. Sleep on the meadows (hotel guests get road permits), or drive up before 09:00 with a parking reservation | Sunrise 06:10 and sunset 20:25 on 15 Aug; 06:49 and 19:27 on 15 Sep |
| **3** | Lago di Braies at 05:30 to 07:00 | Early light before the crowd |
| **4** | Tre Cime at sunset (check shuttle and toll road times) | 20 to 30 minutes before sunset |
| **5** | Cinque Torri early; Gardeccia and the Vajolet Towers in late light | Late afternoon |

**Note:** Lift hours (about 08:30 to 17:30 for Seceda in 2026) mean you must walk or stay in a hut for sunrise and sunset at high viewpoints. No drones in the protected areas.

### Shoulder-season changes

- **June:** Start at lower and mid levels. Check which lifts and huts are open. Snow may remain high.
- **September:** The best balance. Book early for lifts. Restrictions at Braies end on 15 September (2026 date).
- **October:** Choose low-level walks, Törggelen farm inns, and city days. Many lifts and high huts close.

### Ski week (seven nights, Dolomiti Superski)

| Day | Plan |
|---|---|
| **1** | Arrive, buy the lift pass (about €89 per day or €453 for 6 days in high season, 2026/27 reported), hire equipment |
| **2** | Warm-up on local slopes; early night |
| **3** | Sella Ronda: start before 10:00, cross the last pass before about 15:30 (about 6 hours, 44 km) |
| **4** | Another ski area (for example Alta Badia or Kronplatz), depending on your level |

## Common mistakes across all itineraries

- Starting after 10:00 in July or August.
- Planning too many sights per day.
- Moving bases and doing a big walk on the same day.
- Not booking Seceda, Braies, Tre Cime or huts early.
- Not checking the last lift or bus back.
- Having no weather swap.
