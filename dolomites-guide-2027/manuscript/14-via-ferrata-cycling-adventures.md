# 14. Via Ferrata, Cycling and Other Adventures

Beyond walking, the Dolomites offer three activities that visitors ask about most: via ferrata (protected climbing routes), cycling, and family-friendly outdoor days. This chapter explains who each one suits, what it costs, and what people get wrong.

## Via ferrata

### What it is

A *via ferrata* ("iron way") is a mountain route fitted with steel cables, ladders, rungs and sometimes suspension bridges. You clip into the cable with a harness and lanyard. Many routes in the Dolomites began as Great War military paths. Today they let non-climbers reach places that would otherwise need ropes and technical skills.

### Who it suits

- **Suited to:** fit people who are comfortable with heights, sure-footed, and willing to learn the basics.
- **Not suited to:** people with a fear of heights, poor fitness, or anyone who wants to try it without equipment or instruction.
- **Italian grade on trail maps:** EEA (see Chapter 13).

### Difficulty grades

Italy uses letters from **A** (easy) to **D** (extremely difficult). A K-scale (K1 to K6) is also common. Beginners should stay on routes graded A or B (K1 or K2).

| Grade | Meaning for you |
|---|---|
| **A / K1** | Easy. Good for a first route with proper equipment. |
| **B / K2** | Moderate. Some steeper sections. |
| **C / K3** | Hard. Strong fitness and experience, or a guide. |
| **D and above** | Expert only. |

### Equipment

Never go without all three:

1. **A climbing helmet.**
2. **A climbing harness.**
3. **A certified via ferrata lanyard** with an energy absorber (a Y-shaped lanyard with a built-in shock absorber).

Gloves are strongly recommended. Do not improvise with a sling or a rope.

**Hire or buy?** A full set cost about €30 to €50 a day to rent in Cortina in 2026 (some places charged as little as €17). If you plan more than a few days, buying may be cheaper.

### Larger routes

- **Brigata Tridentina** (Passo Gardena to Lago Pisciadù): medium difficulty, about 3 hours, about 700 m of ascent with about 300 m of ferrata, ending at a suspension bridge near Rifugio Cavazza (Chapter 8).
- **Innerkofler–De Luca** (near Tre Cime): grade C, 4 to 5 hours. A guide is advised.
- **Ivano Dibona** (Cristallo, near Cortina): a long expert route of about 9 hours, with a 30-metre suspension bridge at the start.

### Safety

- **July to September** is the main season. Start early and aim to finish by about 13:00 to avoid storms.
- **Never use a via ferrata in a thunderstorm.** The metal cables attract lightning. Turn back at the first sign of bad weather.
- **One person per section** of cable between anchor points. Keep a safe distance behind the person in front.
- **Check the route description,** the grade and the descent. Many routes need a long walk back.
- **Do not go alone** if you are new to it.

### Common mistakes

- Using the wrong gear (a rope sling or a lanyard without an energy absorber).
- Choosing a route by its photo, not its grade.
- Starting late and getting caught by a storm.
- Underestimating the walking time to and from the route.

## Cycling

### Choose your type

| Type | Best for | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Road cycling** | Fit riders who want famous passes | Sella Ronda, Pordoi, Gardena, Campolongo and Giau are the classic climbs |
| **E-bike on roads and paths** | Moderate fitness, longer days | Hire in most villages |
| **Mountain bike and e-MTB** | Trail riders | Bike parks and marked routes |
| **Family cycling** | Children and beginners | Flat valley paths |

### Car-free pass days and events

- **Sellaronda Bike Day.** The four passes (Sella, Gardena, Pordoi, Campolongo) are closed to cars and motorbikes from 08:30 to 16:00 in 2026. The first date for 2027 is reported as **Saturday 5 June**. A second date, usually in September, may follow. The Sella Ronda circuit is about 53 km with about 1,600 m of climbing on bike day.
- **Maratona dles Dolomites.** Race day was reported as **Sunday 4 July 2027**, with event days from 1 to 4 July in Alta Badia. Entry is usually limited. Check the organiser's website early. Roads close on race day, and hotels fill months ahead.
- **Other bike events.** In 2026, Falzarego, Valparola and Campolongo were closed to motor traffic for a morning in June. Check local calendars.

### The Sella Ronda by bike

The mountain-bike route around the Sella is about 57 to 60 km, with about 1,000 m of climbing. Allow 6 to 9 hours with a long lunch. The road route used on Bike Day is about 53 km with about 1,600 m of climbing. Guided e-bike tours with lift help ran about 6 to 7 hours in 2026. See Chapter 8.

### Easy cycle paths

| Path | Length | Level | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dobbiaco to Cortina** | About 33 km | Easy | On the old railway line; about 3 hours |
| **Pusterbike, San Candido to Brunico** | 35 km | Easy | Gentle downhill; return by train |
| **Pusterbike, San Candido to Fortezza** | About 69.8 km | Easy to moderate | A long day |

### E-bike and bike hire

In 2026 in Ortisei, a hardtail e-bike cost about €54 for a half or full day (€64 for one day), and a full-suspension e-bike about €69 to €79. Elsewhere in Selva, Corvara and Canazei, expect about €45 to €75 a day. Book ahead in July and August.

### Bike parks and lifts

- **Kronplatz Bike Park.** The largest trail area in South Tyrol for mountain bikes and e-MTBs. It has nine trails (the longest, Herrnsteig, is 8 km) and lifts that run all summer.
- **Lift transport.** Bikes cost about €5 per bike on many lifts if not included in your ticket. The Seceda lifts charged €7. The SuperSummer card includes bike transport. Some lifts, such as the Boè cable car in Alta Badia, do not carry bikes.

### Rules for mountain bikers

- Stay on marked routes.
- Slow down for walkers and animals.
- Finish before dusk.
- Wear a helmet.

### Common mistakes

- Starting a pass climb in the midday heat.
- Not carrying rain gear and a warm layer.
- Planning a lift-assisted route that includes a lift that does not carry bikes.
- Riding on Sellaronda Bike Day without booking a base nearby.

**Age limits.** The Lagazuoi tunnels are not suitable for children under 8. Many via ferrata operators set minimum ages. Ask when you book.

## Lakes and swimming

Do not assume you can swim in a Dolomites lake.

- **Lago di Carezza.** Swimming is banned to protect the lake.
- **Lago di Anterselva.** Swimming is not permitted inside the nature park, and the water stays very cold.
- **Lago di Braies and Lago di Misurina.** Reports describe the water as too cold for swimming. Check the signs.
- **Lago di Dobbiaco.** Reported as allowing swimming. Check the signs.

Always follow local signs.

## Paragliding, spas and other options

- **Tandem paragliding.** A flight of 15 to 25 minutes cost about €90 to €130 in 2026. Launch sites include Seceda and Spitzbühl on the Alpe di Siusi. It depends on weather and wind, so keep your date flexible.
- **Climbing.** Cinque Torri and other areas offer guided rock climbing. Ask local guide offices for prices and the right route for your skill.
- **Spas.** Many hotels sell day spa passes. One hotel spa listed €68 on weekdays and €100 on weekends and holidays in 2026. Spas are a good plan for bad weather.

## Is it worth the money?

| Activity | Worth it? |
|---|---|
| **Guided via ferrata (first time)** | Yes. Safety and learning are worth the price. |
| **Hiring gear for one day** | Yes. Cheaper than buying. |
| **E-bike hire on a long, hilly day** | Yes, if you are not a strong cyclist. |
| **Tandem paragliding** | A good splurge on a clear day. |
| **Spa day** | Good value for a rainy day. |
| **Paying for lifts you will not use** | No. Count your rides first. |
