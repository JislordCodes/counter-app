# 10. Tre Cime, Sesto, Braies and the Pusteria Valley

This corner of the Dolomites has two of the most famous sights in the range: the Tre Cime di Lavaredo (Drei Zinnen) and Lago di Braies (Pragser Wildsee). It is also the easiest part of the Dolomites to reach by train, which makes it the best choice for visitors without a car.

It is also where booking rules are strictest. Read Chapter 3 before you plan your days here.

## Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Province** | South Tyrol, with the Tre Cime area shared with Veneto on the Auronzo and Misurina side |
| **Main bases** | Sesto (Sexten), Dobbiaco (Toblach), San Candido (Innichen), Villabassa, Braies |
| **Language** | German and Italian |
| **Best for** | Walkers, rail travellers, cyclists, budget-minded visitors, Alta Via 1 starters |
| **Car needed?** | Not essential. A valley railway and buses serve the area, but the booking rules make some days easier with a car. |
| **Nearest airports** | Innsbruck, Venice, Treviso, Bolzano |
| **Season** | Summer lifts and the toll road from late May; huts about June to September |

## Getting there and around

- **By train.** The Pusteria (Val Pusteria) railway runs through Brunico, Dobbiaco and San Candido. Dobbiaco is the main hub for Braies and Tre Cime.
- **By bus.** Lines 442 and 439 serve Lago di Braies; the Tre Cime shuttles are covered below. Each requires a reservation in the peak period.
- **By car.** From Cortina, drive north to Dobbiaco. From the A22, exit at Bressanone/Brixen and follow the valley road.
- **3 Zinnen Mountain Card.** Covers the open cable cars in the 3 Zinnen Dolomites area, with 1-day, 3-of-4, 5-of-7 and 5-of-10-day options. Check prices at dreizinnen.com.

## 1. Tre Cime di Lavaredo loop

| At a glance | |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Three huge limestone towers, the best-known sight in the Dolomites. The classic loop walk circles them from Rifugio Auronzo. |
| **Why visit** | One of the best day walks in Europe, without technical climbing. Good for most fit walkers. |
| **Location and access** | Start at Rifugio Auronzo (2,333 m), reached by toll road, shuttle bus or on foot. See the access table below. |
| **Travel time** | The toll road is 6 to 7 km from Misurina. |
| **Visit time** | The loop is about 9.5 to 10 km and takes 3.5 to 4 hours, including the tunnel detour. Allow a full day with lunch. |
| **Cost (2026)** | Toll road €40 per car. Shuttles about €10 to €22 (see below). Hut meals extra. |
| **Best time** | Early morning or late afternoon. Summer to early autumn. In June or after September, snow may remain, so bring spikes. |
| **Crowd reality** | Very busy from mid-morning in July and August. The car park opens early. |
| **Difficulty** | Easy to moderate. Mostly wide paths with some stony sections. |
| **Photography** | Plan to be at viewpoints 20 to 30 minutes before sunset. Arrive early for fewer people. |
| **Practical tips** | Carry layers and water. Huts on the loop: Auronzo (2,333 m), Lavaredo (2,344 m), Locatelli (2,405 m) and Pian di Cengia (2,528 m). |
| **Common mistakes** | Arriving without a booking, or booking the wrong window. Starting at midday. Overstaying the toll road window. |
| **Nearby** | Cadini di Misurina, Lago di Misurina, Val Fiscalina. |
| **Drawbacks** | Busy, expensive access, and weather that can turn quickly at 2,400 m. |

> **Wish I'd Known:** A toll road booking is for a 12-hour window, one entry only. If you leave and try to return, you will have to pay again. Plan to stay inside your window.

| Option | Cost (2026) | Details |

## 3. Val Fiscalina and Sesto

| At a glance | |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | A long valley with wide paths and huts, leading to the Tre Cime from the north side. |
| **Why visit** | The best car-free way to reach the Tre Cime area on foot. Quieter than the toll road. |
| **Access** | Bus stop and hikers' car park near Hotel Dolomitenhof in Sesto (about 1,455 m). From mid-June to early October, vehicle access beyond Moso is limited from about 09:00 to 16:00; use the bus or walk. |
| **Route to Rifugio Locatelli** | About 13.9 km round trip, about 5 hours walking, about 953 m of climbing. The tourist board calls it a "difficult alpine hut route". |
| **Huts** | Fondovalle hut (1,548 m, May to October) and Rifugio Locatelli (2,405 m, June to September). |
| **Longer option** | Val Fiscalina circuit about 17.8 km and 1,175 m of climbing, about 6.5 hours (8 with stops). |
| **Practical tips** | Start early. Check the bus time for the return. |
| **Common mistakes** | Underestimating the climb. Planning a driving arrival after 09:00. |
| **Drawbacks** | A long day for beginners. |

**The "sundial."** The peaks above Sesto form a natural sundial. Locals read the time from which peak the sun touches. It makes a good photo from the village in the evening.

## 4. Lago di Braies (Pragser Wildsee)

Chapter 3 gives the access rules in detail. In short: in 2026, from 1 July to 15 September, between 09:00 and 16:00, you could drive to the lake only with a reservation. Bus lines 442 and 439 needed an online reservation in that period.

| At a glance | |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | A lake at 1,496 m in a forest setting, with a path around it and rental boats. It is also the start of the Alta Via 1. |
| **Why visit** | One of the most photographed lakes in the Alps, and an easy outing for families. |
| **Visit time** | The lake walk takes about an hour at an easy pace. Allow 2 hours with a boat or a hut stop. |
| **Cost (2026)** | Lakeside parking €40 (P4, pre-booked); other lots from about €13. Boat €55 for 45 minutes private (up to 5 people) or about €20 per person shared. |
| **Best time** | Before 09:00 or after 16:00. September, after 15 September, has no car restrictions. |
| **Crowd reality** | Very busy from mid-morning. |
| **Difficulty** | Easy. |
| **Photography** | Early morning light is calm. Do not fly a drone: the lake lies in the Fanes-Sennes-Braies Nature Park, where drones are banned. |
| **Common mistakes** | Driving up at 10:00. Underestimating how crowded the lake path is at midday. |
| **Drawbacks** | Crowds and fees. |

## 5. Alta Via 1 (the start)

| At a glance | |
|---|---|
| **What it is** | A hut-to-hut trek of about 125 km from Lago di Braies to La Pissa near Belluno. |
| **Time** | Typically 8 to 12 days. |
| **Season** | Mid-June to late September. |
| **Difficulty** | Moderate to challenging. Long ascents and descents, with some narrow or exposed sections and fixed cables. |
| **Booking** | There is no central system. Each hut takes its own bookings. |
| **Cost** | Half board at huts is about €75 to €130 a night (blog estimate). |
| **Practical points** | No baggage transfer is offered on the standard route. Plan to carry everything. |
| **Access** | Buses 445 and 442 from Cortina or Dobbiaco reach Braies. |

## 6. Val Pusteria towns and cycling

- **San Candido (Innichen).** A pretty old town with a historic collegiate church. Base for cycling and the 3 Zinnen area.
- **Dobbiaco (Toblach).** The hub for trains, the Dobbiaco to Cortina cycle path and the Tre Cime shuttle. Lago di Dobbiaco is nearby for an easy lakeside walk.
- **Pusterbike cycle path.** San Candido to Brunico is 35 km. San Candido to Fortezza is about 69.8 km. It is mostly flat or downhill and suits families.
- **Dobbiaco to Cortina cycle path.** About 33 km on the old railway line, easy and suitable for families. Allow about 3 hours.

## Where to stay

For the Tre Cime and Braies, choose **Sesto**, **Dobbiaco** or **San Candido**. They have the train and buses, and prices are lower than in the Val Gardena and Alta Badia resorts. **Misurina** is closer to the toll road but has limited choice. See Chapter 6.

## Where to eat

Locatelli and the other Tre Cime huts serve lunch. In the valley towns, South Tyrolean inns serve dumplings, soups and dishes with speck. Book dinners in July and August. Carry cash for huts.

## Rainy-day options

Museums in San Candido and Dobbiaco, a spa session, and a train ride to Brunico or Bressanone. If the cloud lifts, the Dobbiaco to Cortina cycle path is a good low-level option.

## Common mistakes in this region

- Driving to the Tre Cime without a booking.
- Using a guest pass on the 444 shuttle.
- Arriving at Braies by car at 10:00 in summer.
- Starting the Tre Cime loop after midday.
- Planning to leave the toll road and return within the same slot.

## A simple plan for three days

- **Day 1:** Braies lake before 09:00, then a train or bus to Dobbiaco and the cycle path.
- **Day 2:** Tre Cime loop early, by shuttle or toll road.
- **Day 3:** Val Fiscalina to Locatelli, or a quiet day in San Candido.
