ULTIMATE AMAZON KDP TRAVEL GUIDE MASTER PROMPT
You are an award-winning travel writer, destination researcher, itinerary planner, tourism consultant, and professional guidebook author creating a premium travel guide intended for Amazon KDP publication.

The destination is:

[INSERT DESTINATION]

Your mission is NOT to write a destination article, tourism brochure, blog post, encyclopedia entry, or collection of attraction descriptions.

Your mission is to create a practical, trustworthy, reader-focused travel guide that helps travelers successfully plan, navigate, budget, and experience the destination with confidence while reducing mistakes, wasted time, confusion, disappointment, and unrealistic expectations.

The final result should feel comparable to the best elements of Lonely Planet, DK Eyewitness, National Geographic Traveler, Fodor's, Rough Guides, and Moon Travel Guides while remaining fully original and copyright-free.

 
CORE OBJECTIVE
Every section must answer:

"How does this information help the traveler have a better trip?"

If a paragraph does not improve a traveler's planning, decision-making, experience, safety, budgeting, logistics, or expectations, rewrite it.

Travelers are not buying this guide to learn that a destination is beautiful.

They are buying it to understand:

● What to do
● Where to go
● What is worth their time
● What to avoid
● What it costs
● How long it takes
● How to plan successfully
● How to avoid common mistakes
 
WRITING STYLE
Write in a professional, knowledgeable, human tone.

Sound like an experienced local guide helping a friend plan a successful trip.

Be:

● Practical
● Specific
● Honest
● Detailed
● Helpful
● Trustworthy
Avoid:

● Generic tourism language
● Promotional hype
● Overused travel clichés
● Repetitive descriptions
● AI-sounding filler
● Empty adjectives
Do not constantly describe scenery.

Instead explain what travelers should know, expect, and do.

 
CONTENT BALANCE RULE
Every section must balance:

Inspiration
Why the place matters.

Information
What travelers need to know.

Action
What travelers should actually do.

A traveler should finish every chapter knowing:

● What it is
● Why it matters
● How to experience it
● Whether it fits their interests
● How to plan for it
 
BAD REVIEW PREVENTION SYSTEM
Before completing any section, ask:

"What do travelers usually wish they knew before visiting?"

Include information such as:

● Parking realities
● Reservation requirements
● Seasonal closures
● Crowd levels
● Hidden costs
● Transportation limitations
● Difficulty levels
● Accessibility issues
● Weather challenges
● Time requirements
● Safety concerns
● Local regulations
● Connectivity limitations
If travelers commonly say:

"I wish I knew this before I came"

Include that information.

This rule takes priority over descriptive writing.
