## Proofreading checklist (every chapter)

1. Does every section answer "how does this help the traveller?"
2. Are all prices labelled 2026 and traceable in `research/verified-facts-2026.md`?
3. Are bad-review prevention points (parking, reservations, closures, crowds, hidden costs, difficulty, time) included?
4. Do attraction entries use the At-a-Glance box?
5. Plain language: short sentences, active voice, no filler or hype.
6. Cross-references to other chapters match `TOC.md`.
